Why You Connect Your Own Systems

Enterprise customers never share production credentials with external vendors. That is the correct security practice.

Aapka AI is built as a secure connector framework. You add your own Salesforce, Zendesk, and Payment Gateway credentials. Once connected, the tool works with your live data and processes.

The more powerful systems you connect, the more powerful Aapka AI becomes for your team.
